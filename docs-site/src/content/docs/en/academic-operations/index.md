**Academic Operations** is the area for running academic work: building the curriculum, opening classes, managing course registration, tracking attendance, finalising GPA, and spotting students at risk.

Its four page groups follow the order of a term:

1. **Curriculum Setup** — the framework: terms, programs, units.
2. **Course Delivery** — opening classes, scheduling, registration, retakes, resits.
3. **Attendance & Completion** — attendance tracking and students who failed.
4. **Grades & Performance** — finalising grades, GPA, academic warnings.

Working out of order gets you stuck: no term means no course offering, and no course offering means no student registration.

## Who uses this area

| Role | What they use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Academic office | Terms, programs, units, syllabus templates |
| Academic staff | Opening classes, registration, attendance, failed students, retakes |
| Department head, academic director | Finalising GPA, reviewing warnings, checking term results |
| Support staff | Looking up class or result status when a student asks |

## Every page in this area

| Group | Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curriculum Setup | Academic Terms | Terms and their registration windows |
| Curriculum Setup | Programs | Programs and majors offered |
| Curriculum Setup | Curriculum Versions | The framework applied to each intake |
| Curriculum Setup | Units | Unit catalogue, prerequisites, equivalents |
| Curriculum Setup | Modules | Grouping units into blocks of study |
| Curriculum Setup | Syllabus Templates | Reusable syllabus templates |
| Course Delivery | Course Offering List | Opening course offerings for a term |
| Course Delivery | Class Schedule | Date, time and room for each session |
| Course Delivery | Course Registration | Which students take which offering |
| Course Delivery | Retake Registration | Registering a failed unit again |
| Course Delivery | Thi lại (Exam resit) | Building and tracking the resit list |
| Course Delivery | Lịch thi lại (Resit schedule) | Exam slots, rooms, invigilator assignment |
| Course Delivery | Canvas Courses | Linking an offering to its online course |
| Attendance & Completion | Course Statistics | How each unit is doing this term |
| Attendance & Completion | Attendance Summary | Attendance records by student and session |
| Attendance & Completion | Failed Students | Students who failed and why |
| Grades & Performance | GPA Management | Finalising GPA for a term |
| Grades & Performance | GPA History | Looking up GPA finalised in past terms |
| Grades & Performance | Warning Center | Students on academic or attendance warning |

University-wide reports — Performance Dashboard, Academic Report, Course Ranking — live in the separate **Reports & Audits** menu group.

## Common sequences

| Sequence | Order of pages |
| --- | --- |
| Preparing a new term | Academic Terms → Programs → Curriculum Versions → Units → Syllabus Templates |
| Opening and running classes | Course Offering List → Class Schedule → Course Registration → Canvas Courses |
| Tracking academic risk | Attendance Summary → Failed Students → Warning Center → Retake Registration |
| Running resits | Thi lại → Lịch thi lại |
| Closing the term | Course Statistics → GPA Management → GPA History → Warning Center |
