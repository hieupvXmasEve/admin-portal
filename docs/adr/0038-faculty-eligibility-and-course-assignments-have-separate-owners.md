# Faculty eligibility and course assignments have separate owners

**Status:** accepted

Faculty Workforce owns the Faculty Member's employment and contract state, employee identifier, academic rank, qualifications, expertise, teaching preferences, pay terms, and Teaching Eligibility. Course Delivery & Assessment owns Instructor Assignments, teaching load, timetable, and conflicts, and combines a Workforce eligibility answer with current delivery state when assigning an instructor; Identity & Access separately owns the Lecturer Actor's account and access grant.
