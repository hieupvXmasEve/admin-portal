# Overview

## Current Behavior

Finance Office sidebar navigation was organized by technical objects and pages,
which makes staff choose from charges, invoices, payments, DNG requests, and
exceptions rather than from the work they need to do.

## Target Behavior

Track Task 6 from the Milestone 1 plan as its own story: reorganize Finance
Office into five work groups:

- Hom nay
- Sinh phi
- Thu & Doi soat
- Ngoai le
- Tra cuu & Audit

Keep Discounts & Funding as a separate group because those routes live outside
the Finance module. Student 360 is not a sidebar item.

## Affected Users

- Finance staff navigating the admin/staff app.

## Affected Product Docs

- `docs/features/finance/finance-office-ux-redesign-design.md`
- `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-06-15-finance-office-shell-and-360-foundation.md`

## Portal Impact

Portal impact: none.

This story changes admin/staff sidebar navigation only.

## Non-Goals

- Do not remove existing Finance source pages.
- Do not add Student 360 to the sidebar.
- Do not change permissions on individual menu items beyond preserving existing
  item gates.
